Build a modern web app called “Frontend Interview Flashcards” for web developers preparing for interviews.

The app should feel clean, minimal, fast, and focused for daily study sessions.

Core purpose:
Help users quickly review and memorize frontend interview concepts using categorized flashcards and lightweight spaced repetition.

Target users:

Junior to senior frontend developers
People preparing for React/JavaScript/frontend interviews
Users who want fast review sessions instead of reading long notes
